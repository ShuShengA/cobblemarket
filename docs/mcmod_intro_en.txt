# CobbleMarket —— A Player-to-Player Trading Market for Cobblemon Servers

[![Minecraft](https://img.shields.io/badge/Minecraft-1.21.1-green)](https://modrinth.com/mod/fabric-api)
[![Fabric](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loader-Fabric-orange)](https://fabricmc.net/)
[![NeoForge](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loader-NeoForge-orange)](https://neoforged.net/)

════════════════════════════════════════

CobbleMarket is a player-to-player trading market mod for Cobblemon, available for
both Fabric and NeoForge. Players can buy and sell Pokémon from their party or PC,
and items from their inventory, through three trading channels — direct market
listings, auctions and buy orders — backed by a full market toolkit: listing fees,
automatic expiry returns, seller pending balance, bans and blacklist moderation,
price limits, and transaction history auditing. An optional "Meowth Bank" finance
system (credit loans, installments, deposits, card credentials) is also included.

- Minecraft version: 1.21.1 (Fabric / NeoForge)
- Requirements: Cobblemon (1.8 or later); Fabric additionally needs Fabric API and
  Fabric Language Kotlin, NeoForge additionally needs Kotlin for Forge
  (Architectury is NOT required)
- Optional: CobbleDollars / Cobblemon Economy / Impactor (virtual currency),
  Cobblemon Smartphone (in-game app entry)
- License: GPL-3.0

For the full feature list and every change, see the bundled CHANGELOG.

1. Opening the Market

Three ways to open the market:

- Hotkey K: default key, rebindable in Settings → Key Binds → "Open Market"
- Command: /market gui
- Cobblemon Smartphone: if the smartphone mod is installed, the market entry
  automatically appears in the in-game app list

4. Listing a Pokémon

Click "Sell" on the main screen to open the selection screen:

- Party Pokémon are pinned at the top; PC Pokémon load below with paging, search
  and filters
- Select a Pokémon, enter a price, confirm to list
- A configurable listing fee is charged on listing
- Listings expire after a configurable number of days and automatically enter the
  return list — nothing is ever lost
- At least one Pokémon must remain in the party; active listings per player are
  capped by config

5. Item Market

The item market mirrors the Pokémon market mechanics:

- Search items by name (also by item ID and full tooltip text; items with variants
  such as enchanted books and TMs can be narrowed down to the exact variant)
- Buy any quantity: the buyer chooses an amount and the listing stays up with the
  remaining stock
- Bulk selling: one listing can hold a whole stack of identical items
- Cancel and reclaim: sellers can cancel anytime and the items return to their
  inventory

6. Auction House

Sell Pokémon or items by competitive bidding:

- The seller sets a starting price and a duration; anyone on the server can bid
- When the countdown ends the item is settled automatically to the highest bidder
  and the proceeds go to the seller's pending balance
- A "Mine" tab shows the auctions you have posted; unsold or force-cancelled
  entries are returned automatically — nothing is lost
- Starting price, minimum bid increment and duration are configurable by the
  server owner

7. Buy Orders

Post what you want and let other players deliver it:

- Both Pokémon and items can be requested, with an optional note describing your
  requirements
- The reward is frozen when the order is posted and paid to the deliverer once the
  delivery is confirmed
- Item orders can carry component requirements (for example, only items with a
  specific enchantment); deliveries that do not match are refused
- Orders can be accepted, closed or force-cancelled, and the frozen reward is
  refunded

8. Expired Returns

Expired listings are never lost — they enter your "Expired Returns" list:

- Pokémon listings: claim with one click; the Pokémon returns to your party (or
  the PC if full)
- Item listings: claim with one click; the items return to your inventory
- Partial claims are supported: when storage is full, claim what fits and keep the
  remainder
- The retention period is configurable; unclaimed returns past it are PERMANENTLY
  DELETED, so claim in time

10. Meowth Bank (optional finance system)

The finance system is OFF by default and must be enabled by the server owner; once
enabled, a "Meowth Bank" entry appears on the market entry screen:

- Credit loans: the available limit is computed from the player's trading history,
  loans pay out instantly and repay in installments
- Deposits: idle money earns daily interest, funding the reserve pool for a full
  save-lend loop
- Repayment counter: outstanding loans can be repaid period by period or settled
  early
- Overdue handling: due periods are deducted automatically; insufficient funds mark
  the loan overdue with corresponding restrictions, lifted once it is repaid
- Card credentials: Meow·Purple Gold Card / Meow·Black Gold Card — high-limit
  credentials whose limit and fee discount follow the card tier

Every finance parameter is edited in a dedicated "Meowth Bank Config" screen; the
server owner guide ships with the mod.

11. Admin Tools

The Admin Panel gathers every management feature:

- All Pokémon / item listings: browse and force-cancel any player's listing (search
  by seller supported)
- Auction / buy order management: view all auctions and buy orders and force-cancel
  them
- Ban management: ban or unban players from trading. Bans restrict trading only
  (listing, buying) and NEVER freeze assets — banned players can still cancel their
  listings, claim returns and collect their balance
- Pokémon / item blacklists: keep forbidden species and items out of the market.
  Pokémon support species + IV + form combinations; items are matched down to the
  exact variant (e.g. only one enchantment level). Changes take effect immediately,
  including on existing listings
- Price limits: set minimum and maximum prices for specific items
- Server config: fees, listing caps, durations and switches, all editable in-game
- Full transaction history: server-wide auditing

13. Commands

/market gui                        Open the market entry screen
/market on | /market off           Turn the whole market on / off (emergency switch)
/market ban <player> [duration] [reason]   Ban a player from trading (duration such
                                   as 7d / 12h / 30m, combinable; omit = permanent)
/market unban <player>             Unban a player
/market banlist                    List all active bans
/market card                       Manage card credentials (give / revoke / list)
/market loan clear                 Revoke a player's bad debts
/market reload                     Apply config file edits without restarting
                                   (currency settings still need a restart)

Admin commands are OP-only by default; type /market in game for the full usage.

────────────────────────────────────────

14. Configuration

Config file: config/cobblemarket.json (generated on first launch). Most settings
can be edited in game:

- "Server Config" screen (at the bottom of the admin panel): fees, listing caps and
  durations, return retention, auction and buy order parameters, the market master
  switch
- "Meowth Bank Config" screen (reached from there): every finance parameter

Editing the file by hand works too; /market reload applies the changes without a
restart.

15. Economy

- Currency: an item currency by default (a diamond unless configured otherwise);
  with a virtual currency mod installed you can use Cobblemon Economy (Fabric
  only), CobbleDollars or Impactor instead, with no inventory-space issues
- Fees: charged to the seller on listing (percentage); buyers pay the sticker price
- Pending balance: sale proceeds enter the seller's pending balance and are claimed
  manually; a full inventory never loses money — the remainder stays on the ledger
  until there is room
- Offline earnings: proceeds earned while offline are announced on next login
- Loss-proof fallbacks: any refund or change that cannot be delivered (full
  inventory, etc.) is moved to the pending balance ledger — player money is never
  silently destroyed

────────────────────────────────────────

16. Data Safety

Market data (listings, balances, history) is stored in
world/data/cobblemarket*.dat. The mod has a built-in save self-healing system:

- Auto-verify on startup: every launch verifies file integrity; corrupted files are
  restored from the .bak backup automatically (only changes since the last backup
  are lost)
- Corrupt-file preservation: with no backup available, the damaged file is kept as
  .dat.corrupt for manual recovery and the server starts with fresh data
- Log messages: watch for "restored from backup" (recovered) or "preserved as
  .corrupt" (needs manual attention) in the server log

17. FAQ

Q: The buy screen says "Your party and PC are both full"?
A: Free up party/PC space and buy again. If money was deducted, it is
automatically refunded or moved to your pending balance — never lost.

Q: Can a banned player still get their listings back?
A: Yes. Bans only restrict trading (listing, buying). Cancelling listings,
claiming returns, and collecting balance are always allowed.

Q: Can I buy part of an item listing?
A: Yes. Choose a quantity; the listing remains active with the remaining stock.

Q: When do auction and buy order payments go through?
A: A buy order freezes its reward when posted and pays the deliverer as soon as
the delivery is confirmed. An auction settles to the highest bidder when the
countdown ends, and the proceeds go to the seller's pending balance.

Q: Is the finance system on by default?
A: No — it is off by default, and the "Meowth Bank" entry only appears once the
server owner enables it in the config.

Q: How do I prevent forbidden content on the market?
A: Use the admin panel blacklists and price limits: the Pokémon blacklist supports
species + IV + form combinations, while the item blacklist and price limits match
down to the exact item variant. Both take effect immediately, including on
existing listings.

Q: How long are trade records kept?
A: Recent records are kept in game; CSV ledgers are archived by date under
config/cobblemarket/ for long-term retention.
